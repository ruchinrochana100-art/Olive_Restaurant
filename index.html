Our Story


Back in 2010, the founder of OLIVE, K. Ruchin Rochana had a very simple yet a big dream of having a restaurant whre one will not simply eat the food, they will experience it. Being influenced by the taste of Mediterranean cuisine and my life-long love to cook, I wanted OLIVE to become a place where each meal has a story, each ingredient counts, and each customer will feel like home.The beginning of OLIVE was not an easy task. The ability to source in the freshest ingredients, the highest standards of hygiene and taste ensured, all actions demanded dedication, perseverance, and the faith in the vision. When OLIVE was verified by reputable health agencies, our efforts were rewarded as the move boosted our quality and safety.


However, it was true people that are at the center of OLIVE. All the tables, all the get togethers, all the mutual laughs make us remember the reason why we started this journey to unite, to party, and make memorable moments with food.The OLIVE is a monument of that love and struggle today. We are inviting you to come and experience the love and care in each meal and become a part of a story which is constantly growing with every visitor that comes to visit us.
